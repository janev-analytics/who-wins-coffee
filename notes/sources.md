## Fiscal calendar notes

**Starbucks: 52/53-week fiscal year**
- FY ends on the Sunday closest to Sep 30. Quarters are 13 weeks, not calendar months.
- A 52-week year is 364 days, so the FY end moves 1 day earlier each year (2 in a leap year):
  Oct 3, 2021 → Oct 2, 2022 → Oct 1, 2023 → Sep 29, 2024 → Sep 28, 2025.
- Every ~5–6 years a 53rd week is added to pull the date back.
  FY2021 (Sep 28, 2020 – Oct 3, 2021) = 53 weeks → ~2% extra sales. Verify in the 10-K.

**RBI: calendar quarters**
- Periods end on the last day of the month (10-Q for the period ending Sep 30, 2026).
- FY end: Dec 31 (calendar year). Reports in USD.
- Segments: 6. Use **TH = Tim Hortons in Canada and the U.S.** only (matches Starbucks North America). Tims outside North America sits in INTL; excluded.

**Dutch Bros:** FY = calendar year (Dec 31). Reports in **USD thousands** (SBUX and RBI report in millions; convert before comparing).
1,136 shops at Dec 31, 2025: 811 company-operated, 325 franchised, 25 U.S. states.

**How I handle it**
- Starbucks FY and RBI FY cover different months (~3-month offset). I note this, not hide it.
- Compare ratios (margins, growth rates, sales per store), not raw totals.
- Flag 53-week years; draw no conclusions from their growth spike.
