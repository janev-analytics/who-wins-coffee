# Who Wins Coffee?

**Question:** Can a fast grower like Dutch Bros beat Starbucks at its own game?
Starbucks is closing stores and its margins are falling; Dutch Bros is opening stores and growing ~28% a year. Who is winning in the U.S., and can it last?

| Company | Role | Model |
|---|---|---|
| Starbucks (SBUX) | The incumbent | Mostly company-operated cafés; North America segment |
| Dutch Bros (BROS) | The challenger | Drive-thru, ~71% company-operated, U.S. only |
| Tim Hortons (RBI) | Bonus episode | Franchised: "what if someone else paid for the stores?" |

**Data:** annual 10-K filings from SEC EDGAR.

**Status:** Week 1. Starbucks FY2023–FY2025 model done (`excel/`); Dutch Bros next.
